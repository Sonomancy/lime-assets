Kixie PowerCall Storage Cleaner: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

What the extension does

Kixie PowerCall Storage Cleaner is an internal tool published by Lime Online. When the user clicks Yes in its popup, it closes Kixie PowerCall windows and deletes stored data for the Kixie PowerCall extension and for https://app.kixie.com.

Data collection

The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any user data. It has no servers and makes no network requests.

To do its job, it reads the list of installed extensions (to find Kixie PowerCall) and the URLs of open tabs (to find and close Kixie PowerCall windows). This happens only on the user's device, only after the user clicks Yes, and the information is never saved or sent anywhere.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact Lime Online IT.
